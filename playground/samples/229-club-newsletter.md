Ashbyford Birdwatching Club: the September newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Pavel Kowalczyk, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 160 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on 2 February 2027 at the Beacon Lane grounds in Kestrelcross. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 178 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 20 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by 28 January 2027 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 17, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 4 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Dates for the diary. The monthly walk starts at ten o'clock on the first Saturday and covers about 50 kilometres. The committee meets on the second Wednesday of the month. A repair weekend is planned for 1 May 2027, and a social evening for 22 April 2027. All the dates will be repeated on the noticeboard and on the club's web page, so please check them before you travel.

Member profile. This month we meet Oskar Jankowski, who joined the club 50 years ago and has not missed a single season since. Asked what keeps him coming back, he said that it is the people, the fresh air and the tea at the end. He has a favourite spot near the old tree, and he has promised to show new members around.

Tips of the month. Bring a spare pair of dry socks, since the paths can be wet. Check the weather before you leave and tell somebody where you are going. Water is available at the clubhouse, but not along the way. If you are bringing a child, please let us know in advance so that we have enough helpers and equipment for the 50 youngest members.

Thank you. Special thanks to Oskar Jankowski for painting the fence, to Tilda Whitlock for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 443 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Ironbark Engineering, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

This newsletter was put together by Felix Whitlock. If you have an item for next time, send it in before the end of the month.
