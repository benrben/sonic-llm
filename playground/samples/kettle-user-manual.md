# Brewmate K2 smart kettle: user guide

Congratulations on your new Brewmate K2 kettle. Please read this guide before the first use and keep it for later reference.
The K2 is an electric kettle with a 1.7 litre capacity, five temperature presets and a keep-warm function.

## In the box

- Brewmate K2 kettle with a stainless steel body
- 360-degree power base with a 1.0 metre cord
- Removable limescale filter (already fitted in the spout)
- This guide and a two-year warranty card

## Before the first use

1. Remove all stickers and packing material from inside the kettle.
2. Fill the kettle with water up to the MAX line, boil it once and pour the water away. Do this twice to clear any
   manufacturing residue. The water from the first two boils is not for drinking.
3. Place the power base on a flat, dry, heat-resistant surface, at least 10 cm away from walls and cabinets.

## Using the kettle

Fill the kettle between the MIN line (0.5 litres) and the MAX line (1.7 litres). Never use it with less than the minimum, because
the heating element may be exposed and the protective cut-off will trigger. Close the lid until it clicks, place the kettle on
the base and press the temperature button until the light shows the setting you want:

| Preset | Temperature | Good for |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | 70 degrees C | baby formula (after the water has cooled to the right level) and delicate green tea |
| 2 | 80 degrees C | green and white tea |
| 3 | 90 degrees C | coffee and oolong tea |
| 4 | 95 degrees C | herbal teas and instant noodles |
| 5 | 100 degrees C | black tea and general use |

Press the start button. The kettle beeps once when it reaches the temperature. A full kettle takes about 4 minutes to reach 100
degrees. If you press the keep-warm button after boiling, the kettle holds the temperature for up to 30 minutes and then switches
itself off.

## Cleaning and descaling

Always unplug the kettle and let it cool before cleaning. Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Never immerse the kettle or the
base in water, and do not put any part in the dishwasher.

Remove limescale every month if you live in a hard water area, and every three months if the water is soft. To descale, fill the
kettle halfway with water and add 50 ml of white vinegar or one tablespoon of citric acid. Boil it, leave it for 20 minutes, then
pour it away and rinse the kettle twice with clean water. The limescale filter in the spout can be pulled out and rinsed under the
tap.

## Troubleshooting

- **The light does not come on.** Check that the plug is in the socket and the kettle is fully seated on the base.
- **The kettle switches off before boiling.** There may be too little water in it. Fill it above the MIN line.
- **The water tastes of plastic.** This is normal for the first few uses; boil and discard two more full kettles.
- **Loud noise while heating.** Limescale on the element causes this. Descale the kettle.
- **The kettle leaks.** Stop using it at once and contact customer support. Do not try to open the housing.

## Safety

Keep the kettle out of reach of children. The outside of the spout and the lid become hot. Do not open the lid while the kettle is
boiling. Use only the base that came with the kettle. If the cord is damaged, it must be replaced by the manufacturer or a
qualified person.

## Warranty and support

The kettle comes with a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase, covering faults in materials and workmanship. It does
not cover damage from limescale or from use without water. Keep your receipt. Support is available on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00.
