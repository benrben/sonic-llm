Utilities and services. Water and the shared electricity in the hallway are included in the heating charge. The tenant arranges and pays for their own electricity and internet, and must give the meter readings on the day they move in; the starting reading on the electricity meter was 919. Rubbish collection is on 17 days of the month, and bins must be put out the evening before.

Parking and storage. There is room for one bicycle per person in the shared room on the ground floor, and a small cellar compartment, number 17, belongs to the flat. No parking space is included, but residents may apply to the town for a permit. The landlord asks that nothing is stored on the stairs or in the corridor because of the fire rules.

Insurance. The landlord insures the building and the shared parts. The tenant is advised to take out a contents insurance policy and to include cover for accidental damage and water. A copy of the policy should be handed to the landlord on request. A neighbour, Tilda Whitlock, has agreed to keep a spare key for emergencies, and the landlord knows about this arrangement.
