Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 31 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 872 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 31 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 46 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Pavel Jankowski each spring.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Cinderlake Studio offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Pavel Jankowski, who lives in Fenwickfield, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Social events. The summer outing to Fenwickfield went well, with 31 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Pavel Jankowski suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.
