Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of PM-4377 is 269 euros, not 408 euros.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Tilda Haddad, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Tilda Haddad will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 86 years.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 86 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.
