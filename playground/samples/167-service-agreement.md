Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-6122 is 12 euros, not 133 euros.

Ending the lease. At the end, the tenant returns all keys, cleans the flat and sets up a time for the final inspection with the landlord. Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 55 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.

Parking and storage. There is room for one bicycle per person in the shared room on the ground floor, and a small cellar compartment, number 55, belongs to the flat. No parking space is included, but residents may apply to the town for a permit. The landlord asks that nothing is stored on the stairs or in the corridor because of the fire rules.

Insurance. The landlord insures the building and the shared parts. The tenant is advised to take out a contents insurance policy and to include cover for accidental damage and water. A copy of the policy should be handed to the landlord on request. A neighbour, Vera Castellan, has agreed to keep a spare key for emergencies, and the landlord knows about this arrangement.

Changes and repairs. The tenant may paint the walls in light colours if the flat is returned in white at the end. Drilling holes for shelves is allowed, but large nails and sticky hooks on tiles are not. The flat has 55 windows, all of which were checked by the landlord before the tenant moved in. Broken glass from normal use is the tenant's cost.
