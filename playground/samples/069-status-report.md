Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-6538 is 61 euros, not 246 euros.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 28 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-722.

Team notes. A new colleague, Sven Iversen, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 28 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.

Communication. The weekly status is sent to the steering group every Friday. The team keeps a public board where everybody can see what is in progress. If something blocks the work for more than a day, it is raised in the daily check-in and, if needed, with the steering group. Next month we will hold a short review to see how the project is going overall.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Sven Iversen, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 28 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Lanternworks Foods, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 28 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.
