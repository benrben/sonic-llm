Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-5844 is 447 euros, not 123 euros.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 38th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 20 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Irene Okonkwo will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 38 years.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 38 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.
