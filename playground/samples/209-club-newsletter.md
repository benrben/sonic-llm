Tarnfield Swimming Club: the October newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Pavel Varga, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 206 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on March 22, 2027 at the Orchard Lane grounds in Corvinport. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 183 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 31 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by March 10, 2027 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 13, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 5 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Dates for the diary. The monthly walk starts at ten o'clock on the first Saturday and covers about 27 kilometres. The committee meets on the second Wednesday of the month. A repair weekend is planned for March 23, 2027, and a social evening for January 26, 2027. All the dates will be repeated on the noticeboard and on the club's web page, so please check them before you travel.

Tips of the month. Bring a spare pair of dry socks, since the paths can be wet. Check the weather before you leave and tell somebody where you are going. Water is available at the clubhouse, but not along the way. If you are bringing a child, please let us know in advance so that we have enough helpers and equipment for the 27 youngest members.

Thank you. Special thanks to Jonas Rossetti for painting the fence, to Leon Laurent for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 507 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Tidewater Logistics, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

Member profile. This month we meet Jonas Rossetti, who joined the club 27 years ago and has not missed a single season since. Asked what keeps him coming back, he said that it is the people, the fresh air and the tea at the end. He has a favourite spot near the old tree, and he has promised to show new members around.

This newsletter was put together by Yara Varga. If you have an item for next time, send it in before the end of the month.
