Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-7533 is 60 euros, not 402 euros.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Dario Brandt will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 79 years.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 79th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 49 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 79 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.
