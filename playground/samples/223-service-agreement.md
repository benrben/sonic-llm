Utilities and services. Water and the shared electricity in the hallway are included in the heating charge. The tenant arranges and pays for their own electricity and internet, and must give the meter readings on the day they move in; the starting reading on the electricity meter was 420. Rubbish collection is on 6 days of the month, and bins must be put out the evening before.

Ending the lease. At the end, the tenant returns all keys, cleans the flat and sets up a time for the final inspection with the landlord. Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 6 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.

Parking and storage. There is room for one bicycle per person in the shared room on the ground floor, and a small cellar compartment, number 6, belongs to the flat. No parking space is included, but residents may apply to the town for a permit. The landlord asks that nothing is stored on the stairs or in the corridor because of the fire rules.
