Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-8726 is 289 euros, not 466 euros.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Oskar Tavares will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 44 years.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Oskar Tavares, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 44 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.
