Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of PM-7813 is 344 euros, not 177 euros.

Wish list. If there is time we would like to see the lighthouse, walk the hill path to the ruined castle, and find a shop that sells local ceramics. Mira Haddad recommended the sunset from the old pier, and a small gallery that is open until 7 in the evening. We will decide day by day and not plan every hour.

Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 430, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.

Getting around. The old town is small and easy to walk. A day ticket for local buses costs 64 euros, and the ticket machine at the station takes cards. Bicycles can be rented near the harbour, but the hills are steeper than they look. Mira Haddad, who visited last year, warns that the cobbled streets are hard on suitcase wheels.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Wrenfield Foods near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 64 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Norwickham often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.
