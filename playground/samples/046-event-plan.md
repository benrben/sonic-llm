Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-9132 is 444 euros, not 191 euros.

Packing. We will take light clothes that can be layered, a rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and one nicer outfit for the evening. The weather forecast promises around 23 degrees in the afternoon and cooler nights, with a chance of rain on two days. Sun cream, a small first aid kit, a reusable water bottle and an adapter for the sockets also go in the bag.

Wish list. If there is time we would like to see the lighthouse, walk the hill path to the ruined castle, and find a shop that sells local ceramics. Zoran Fontaine recommended the sunset from the old pier, and a small gallery that is open until 8 in the evening. We will decide day by day and not plan every hour.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Wrenfield Systems near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 77 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Ostercross often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.
