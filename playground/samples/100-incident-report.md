Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-3992 is 461 euros, not 269 euros.

Metrics. The service normally answers in about 56 milliseconds and fails fewer than 51 requests in a thousand. During the incident the failure rate rose sharply and the average answer time went up more than tenfold. Within 25 minutes of the fix being applied, both numbers returned to normal values. Graphs of the period are attached to the ticket.

Costs. The incident used about 56 hours of engineering time in total, spread over several people. We offered no refunds, since nobody was charged for the time of the outage. The cost of the extra servers we started during the recovery was small, and they were shut down again the following day. The review meeting will take one hour.

Communication. The status page was updated three times, and each update was reviewed by Jonas Tennant before it was published. An internal message went to the wider team, and a short note was sent to customers who had contacted support. We believe the updates were honest and clear, although one of them used a technical term that customers did not know.
