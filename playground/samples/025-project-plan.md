Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-8476 is 272 euros, not 466 euros.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Vantage Foods, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 36 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Hugo Marchetti, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 36 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Communication. The weekly status is sent to the steering group every Friday. The team keeps a public board where everybody can see what is in progress. If something blocks the work for more than a day, it is raised in the daily check-in and, if needed, with the steering group. Next month we will hold a short review to see how the project is going overall.
