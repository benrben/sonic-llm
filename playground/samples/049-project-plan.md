Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-3025 is 148 euros, not 170 euros.

Team notes. A new colleague, Karin Rossetti, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 37 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 37 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-532.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Saltmarsh Freight, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 37 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.
