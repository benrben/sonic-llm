Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 687, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.

Packing. We will take light clothes that can be layered, a rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and one nicer outfit for the evening. The weather forecast promises around 26 degrees in the afternoon and cooler nights, with a chance of rain on two days. Sun cream, a small first aid kit, a reusable water bottle and an adapter for the sockets also go in the bag.

Getting around. The old town is small and easy to walk. A day ticket for local buses costs 53 euros, and the ticket machine at the station takes cards. Bicycles can be rented near the harbour, but the hills are steeper than they look. Dario Ferreira, who visited last year, warns that the cobbled streets are hard on suitcase wheels.
