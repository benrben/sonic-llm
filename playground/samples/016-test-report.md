Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-5363 is 236 euros, not 274 euros.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 20th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 13 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 20 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 20 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.
