Social events. The summer outing to Fenwickfield went well, with 51 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Wim Whitlock suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 51 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

The hall. The caretaker, Wim Whitlock, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 51 chairs and 3 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.
