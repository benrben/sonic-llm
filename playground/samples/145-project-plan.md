Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-7223 is 303 euros, not 253 euros.

Team notes. A new colleague, Ulrich Oyelaran, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 85 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Ulrich Oyelaran, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 85 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Applegate Labs, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 85 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.
