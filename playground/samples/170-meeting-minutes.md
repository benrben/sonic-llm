Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-3532 is 210 euros, not 277 euros.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 61 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Social events. The summer outing to Quarryburg went well, with 61 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Marta Wexler suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Saltmarsh Print offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Marta Wexler, who lives in Quarryburg, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 714 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 61 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 74 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Marta Wexler each spring.
