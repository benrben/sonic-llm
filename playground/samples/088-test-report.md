Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-3442 is 213 euros, not 269 euros.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 94 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 94 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Rafael Lindqvist, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.
