Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of PM-3595 is 234 euros, not 20 euros.

The hall. The caretaker, Felix Ferreira, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 75 chairs and 82 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 75 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Social events. The summer outing to Norwickford went well, with 75 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Felix Ferreira suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Cinderlake Textiles offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Felix Ferreira, who lives in Norwickford, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.
