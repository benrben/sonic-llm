Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of PM-2726 is 386 euros, not 468 euros.

Packing. We will take light clothes that can be layered, a rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and one nicer outfit for the evening. The weather forecast promises around 18 degrees in the afternoon and cooler nights, with a chance of rain on two days. Sun cream, a small first aid kit, a reusable water bottle and an adapter for the sockets also go in the bag.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Quillon Print near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 6 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Ashbycross often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.

Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 937, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.

Wish list. If there is time we would like to see the lighthouse, walk the hill path to the ruined castle, and find a shop that sells local ceramics. Olga Jankowski recommended the sunset from the old pier, and a small gallery that is open until 10 in the evening. We will decide day by day and not plan every hour.
