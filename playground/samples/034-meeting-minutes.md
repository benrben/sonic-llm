The hall. The caretaker, Olga Marchetti, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 90 chairs and 47 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 90 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 344 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 90 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 47 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Olga Marchetti each spring.
