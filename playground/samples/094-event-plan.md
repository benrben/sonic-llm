Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 157, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Tidewater Logistics near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 38 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Norwickholm often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.

Packing. We will take light clothes that can be layered, a rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and one nicer outfit for the evening. The weather forecast promises around 16 degrees in the afternoon and cooler nights, with a chance of rain on two days. Sun cream, a small first aid kit, a reusable water bottle and an adapter for the sockets also go in the bag.
