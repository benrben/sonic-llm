Ivoryfield Birdwatching Club: the June newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Ulrich Zielinski, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 327 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on January 22, 2027 at the Mill Lane grounds in Tarncross. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 108 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 23 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by December 28, 2026 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 17, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 4 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Member profile. This month we meet Jonas Tavares, who joined the club 19 years ago and has not missed a single season since. Asked what keeps him coming back, he said that it is the people, the fresh air and the tea at the end. He has a favourite spot near the old tree, and he has promised to show new members around.

Lost and found. Over the summer the following items were left at the clubhouse: a blue water bottle, one glove, a pair of reading glasses and a folded umbrella. They are in a box by the door. Anything that is not collected within 19 weeks will go to the charity shop in Larkinridge. Please look in the box, even if you think you have lost nothing.

Thank you. Special thanks to Jonas Tavares for painting the fence, to Celia Jankowski for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 871 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Copperleaf Systems, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

Dates for the diary. The monthly walk starts at ten o'clock on the first Saturday and covers about 19 kilometres. The committee meets on the second Wednesday of the month. A repair weekend is planned for March 11, 2027, and a social evening for April 6, 2027. All the dates will be repeated on the noticeboard and on the club's web page, so please check them before you travel.

This newsletter was put together by Dario Abernethy. If you have an item for next time, send it in before the end of the month.
