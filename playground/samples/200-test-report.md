Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-9251 is 360 euros, not 296 euros.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 66th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 94 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 66 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Amara Jankowski will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 66 years.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Amara Jankowski, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.
