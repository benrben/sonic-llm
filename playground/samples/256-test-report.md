Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-3611 is 215 euros, not 168 euros.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 36 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Pavel Castellan will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 36 years.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 36 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.
