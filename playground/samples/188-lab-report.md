Laboratory report, batch BT-5460

Purpose
This report describes the routine check of water samples from the east pond, taken to confirm that the acidity and the dissolved mineral content stay within the limits agreed with the site manager. The work was carried out by Anouk Rossetti and reviewed by Karin Thorne.

Samples
17 samples were collected on April 10, 2027 in clean, labelled bottles and brought to the laboratory within two hours. The reference sample, labelled SM-177, was kept at the same temperature as the others and treated in exactly the same way. The room temperature during the measurements was 21 degrees Celsius.

Method
Each sample was shaken gently, left to settle for five minutes and then measured with the Nanex 826. The instrument was calibrated before the first sample using two standard solutions, and checked again after the last one; the drift was below the tolerance. Every sample was measured 4 times and the average was written down. A blank sample of distilled water gave a reading of 8.7, which was subtracted where needed.

Results
The average acidity of the samples was pH 6.7. The mineral content of the reference sample SM-177 was 14.2 mg/L. The other samples were close to it, and none of them stood out from the group. There were no signs of contamination, and no bottle was cloudy or had a visible film on the surface.

Discussion
The acidity is typical for this kind of pond and gives no cause for concern. The mineral content is a little lower than in the spring, which is expected after a wet period. We recommend repeating the check next quarter and keeping the same instrument for the measurement, so that the numbers can be compared with confidence.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 98 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Vera Haddad will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 98 years.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 98th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 84 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Vera Haddad, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.

Notes
Bottles and equipment were washed and rinsed with distilled water. Waste solutions were collected in the marked container. The raw numbers are stored in the laboratory notebook, and a copy of this report was handed to the site manager.
