Hearthly desk lamp, model KX-196: user guide

Thank you for choosing the Hearthly desk lamp. Please read this guide before you use it for the first time and keep it somewhere safe. The model number is printed on the label under the base; you will need it if you call the help line.

Setting up
Place the desk lamp on a flat, dry surface at least 29 cm from walls and other appliances. Do not put it near a sink or on a soft cloth. Remove the stickers and the foam from the box, and wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Plug it into a standard wall socket. The desk lamp draws 1245 watts, so avoid sharing a single extension lead with other heavy appliances.

Everyday use
Press the round button once to start and once to stop. The light turns green while it is working and white when it is waiting. The tank holds 4 litres and should not be filled above the marked line. Switch it off and unplug it before you clean it, and let it cool for about 32 minutes after a long session. Never use harsh chemicals on the casing; mild soapy water is enough.

If something goes wrong
If the display shows E-85, the filter or the inlet is blocked: switch off, unplug, clean the filter under running water and try again. The code E-38 means that the unit has overheated and is cooling down; wait until the light stops flashing. The code E-21 means the tank is empty or has been removed. Other codes are explained on the support page.

To reset the desk lamp to its factory settings, hold the round button for 8 seconds until the light blinks twice. This clears your saved preferences but does not affect the warranty.

Warranty and support
Your desk lamp is covered by a warranty of 28 months from the date of purchase, as long as it has been used as described in this guide. Keep your receipt with this booklet. For help, call the support line on extension 307 during office hours, or write to the address in the box. Please have the model number ready. Repairs made by anyone other than an approved service centre are not covered.

Accessories. A spare filter costs 48 euros and can be ordered with the part code RF-131 from the shop or from any approved dealer. A carrying bag and a second power cord are also available. Only use accessories made for this product; others may not fit well and can cause damage. The packaging contains 56 small screws for the wall bracket and a short instruction sheet.

Safety. Use the product only indoors and only for the purpose described in this guide. Keep it out of the reach of children under 14 years. Do not let the cord hang over the edge of a table. If the cord or the plug is damaged, stop using the product and have it replaced by the service centre. Never open the casing yourself: there are no parts inside that you can repair, and it may void the warranty.

Frequently asked questions. Why does the product make a low humming sound? This is normal and comes from the motor. Why does it smell a little when new? A new product can give off a mild smell for the first 48 hours of use, which disappears with ventilation. Can I use it in a bathroom? We do not recommend this, because moisture and electricity do not mix well.

Environment. The packaging is made from recycled cardboard and can be recycled again. The product uses little energy when it is waiting, about 48 percent of its working power. We design our products to last, and replacement parts are kept for several years after a model is sold. Please do not throw batteries or electronics into the household waste.

Disposal
When the desk lamp reaches the end of its life, take it to a local collection point for electrical items. Do not put it in the household bin.
