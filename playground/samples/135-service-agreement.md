Changes and repairs. The tenant may paint the walls in light colours if the flat is returned in white at the end. Ending the lease. At the end, the tenant returns all keys, cleans the flat and sets up a time for the final inspection with the landlord. Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 85 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.
