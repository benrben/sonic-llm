Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-4842 is 207 euros, not 413 euros.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Ironbark Supply, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 57 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Team notes. A new colleague, Olga Brandt, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 57 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.

Communication. The weekly status is sent to the steering group every Friday. The team keeps a public board where everybody can see what is in progress. If something blocks the work for more than a day, it is raised in the daily check-in and, if needed, with the steering group. Next month we will hold a short review to see how the project is going overall.
