Summary of the residential lease at 10 Harbour Lane, flat 2, Hollisford

This summary explains the main points of the lease in plain language. If this summary and the signed lease disagree, the signed lease is the one that counts.

The people involved
The landlord is Tilda Ulbricht. The tenant is Hugo Kessler. The landlord lives in the same town and can be reached at the number written in the lease.

Money
The monthly rent is 1135 euros, paid in advance on the first working day of each month by standing order. A security deposit of 1773 euros was paid before the keys were handed over. It will be returned within a month after the tenant leaves, less any fair deduction for damage beyond normal wear. In addition to the rent, the tenant pays about 71 euros a month toward heating, which is reviewed once a year against the real bills. If the rent is more than ten days late, the landlord may charge a fee of 52 euros.

Length of the lease
The lease starts on 28 February 2027 and runs for 12 months. After that period it continues from month to month unless either side ends it. Either side can end the lease by giving notice in writing; the notice period is 73 days. The tenant may not end the lease during the first three months except by agreement.

Rules of the house
The flat is let for living in and not for business. A pet may be kept only with the landlord's written permission. Smoking is not allowed inside. The tenant must keep the stairs and the shared bicycle room tidy on the weeks set out in the cleaning rota, and must not make loud noise after ten at night. Small repairs, such as changing light bulbs, are the tenant's job; larger repairs should be reported to the landlord at once.

Parking and storage. There is room for one bicycle per person in the shared room on the ground floor, and a small cellar compartment, number 34, belongs to the flat. No parking space is included, but residents may apply to the town for a permit. The landlord asks that nothing is stored on the stairs or in the corridor because of the fire rules.

Utilities and services. Water and the shared electricity in the hallway are included in the heating charge. The tenant arranges and pays for their own electricity and internet, and must give the meter readings on the day they move in; the starting reading on the electricity meter was 414. Rubbish collection is on 34 days of the month, and bins must be put out the evening before.

Ending the lease. At the end, the tenant returns all keys, cleans the flat and sets up a time for the final inspection with the landlord. Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 34 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.

Insurance. The landlord insures the building and the shared parts. The tenant is advised to take out a contents insurance policy and to include cover for accidental damage and water. A copy of the policy should be handed to the landlord on request. A neighbour, Greta Zielinski, has agreed to keep a spare key for emergencies, and the landlord knows about this arrangement.

Inspection
The landlord may inspect the flat with at least two days' notice and at a reasonable time. A photo list of the condition of each room was signed on the day of the handover, and both people kept a copy.
