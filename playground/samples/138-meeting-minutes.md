A short email from Tilda Pellegrini, who lives in Sablebridge, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

The hall. The caretaker, Tilda Pellegrini, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 40 chairs and 86 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 905 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 40 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 86 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Tilda Pellegrini each spring.

Social events. The summer outing to Sablebridge went well, with 40 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Tilda Pellegrini suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 40 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.
