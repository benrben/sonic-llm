Social events. The summer outing to Brindlehaven went well, with 3 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 3 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Copperleaf Freight offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 905 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 3 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 58 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Clara Marchetti each spring.

The hall. The caretaker, Clara Marchetti, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 3 chairs and 58 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended.
