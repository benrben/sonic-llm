Reading Circle of Quarrystead: minutes of the committee meeting

The committee met on 5 January 2027 at the hall on Quay Lane, Quarrycross. Tomas Ulbricht took the chair and Wim Rasmussen kept the minutes. 12 members came, which was well above the quorum of 5. Apologies were received from three people who could not travel. The minutes of the meeting held on 9 November 2026 were read, agreed as a true record and signed.

1. Matters arising
The clerk reported that the letter to the council about the hall booking had been sent and acknowledged. Nothing further was needed. The chair thanked everyone who helped to tidy the hall after the last event, and asked that the cupboard in room 2 be kept clear so that the cleaners can reach the sockets.

2. Discussion of the autumn programme
Members spoke for about forty minutes on the autumn programme. Several people felt that the plan was sound but that the timetable was too tight, and a smaller group offered to look at costs before the next meeting. The chair reminded the meeting that the club spent 659 euros last year on similar work and that this figure had been agreed as a guide rather than a limit. After a short vote the committee approved a budget of 3986 euros for the autumn programme, to be spent only against receipts.

3. Actions
Jakob Tavares will collect three written quotes and send them to the clerk by 27 January 2027. The clerk will add the quotes to the next agenda. Nobody else needs to do anything before then. It was agreed that any member may ask to see the receipts at any time, and that the treasurer will keep them in the blue folder at the hall.

4. Any other business
A member asked whether the club could offer a short beginners' session in the spring. The chair said this was a good idea and asked for a volunteer to write a proposal; no one volunteered yet, so the item stays open. There was a brief discussion of the refreshments rota, which works well, and of the noticeboard, which needs a new lock.

Social events. The summer outing to Quarryport went well, with 61 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Marta Rossetti suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Applegate Freight offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Marta Rossetti, who lives in Quarryport, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

The hall. The caretaker, Marta Rossetti, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 61 chairs and 91 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 564 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 61 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 91 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Marta Rossetti each spring.

The meeting closed at ten past nine. The next meeting will be held on 19 February 2027.
