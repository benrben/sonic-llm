Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-3047 is 111 euros, not 190 euros.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 73th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 33 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Comments from the reviewer. The report is clear and the numbers are easy to follow. The reviewer asked to add the temperature of the samples at the time of collection, and a short sentence about the weather on that day, because rain can change the readings. Faruk Dvorak will keep a copy of the raw data for at least 73 years.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 73 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Faruk Dvorak, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.

Background. The east pond was dug many years ago and is fed by rain and by a small stream. It is used for watering the garden and by wildlife, and it has been sampled by the site team every quarter for the last 73 years. The notebook from the first year shows slightly different readings, but the method was not the same.
