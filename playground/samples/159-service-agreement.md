Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 25 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.

Changes and repairs. The tenant may paint the walls in light colours if the flat is returned in white at the end.
