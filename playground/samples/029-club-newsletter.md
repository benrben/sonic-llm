Marlowham Swimming Club: the March newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Olga Varga, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 98 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on 26 January 2027 at the Willow Lane grounds in Kestrelbridge. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 50 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 8 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by 7 January 2027 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 12, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 4 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Tips of the month. Bring a spare pair of dry socks, since the paths can be wet. Check the weather before you leave and tell somebody where you are going. Water is available at the clubhouse, but not along the way. If you are bringing a child, please let us know in advance so that we have enough helpers and equipment for the 26 youngest members.

Member profile. This month we meet Tilda Whitlock, who joined the club 26 years ago and has not missed a single season since. Asked what keeps him coming back, he said that it is the people, the fresh air and the tea at the end. He has a favourite spot near the old tree, and he has promised to show new members around.

Thank you. Special thanks to Tilda Whitlock for painting the fence, to Viktor Laurent for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 958 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Northwind Logistics, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

Lost and found. Over the summer the following items were left at the clubhouse: a blue water bottle, one glove, a pair of reading glasses and a folded umbrella. They are in a box by the door. Anything that is not collected within 26 weeks will go to the charity shop in Quarrystead. Please look in the box, even if you think you have lost nothing.
