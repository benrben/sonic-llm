# Three days in Lisbon: our plan for the first week of May

Travellers: Ana, Rui and their daughter Beatriz (9). We fly Thursday 2 May and return Sunday 5 May. This page is the plan we
agreed on and the things we still need to book.

## Getting there and around

The flight lands at 11:25. The airport metro (red line) goes to the city centre in about 25 minutes and a single ticket costs
1.85 euros, plus 0.50 euros for the reusable card. We decided against a taxi. A 24-hour transit ticket costs 6.80 euros and
covers metro, trams and buses, so we will buy one each day we plan a lot of travel. Beatriz travels for free until she is 12 on
buses and trams but needs a ticket on the metro.

We are staying in a small apartment in Alfama, a ten-minute walk below the castle, 95 euros a night for three nights. Check-in
is from 14:00 and the host will keep our bags if we arrive early. The streets are steep and cobbled, so we are packing light
bags with soft sides, not hard suitcases.

## Day 1, Thursday: Alfama and the river

- 13:00 lunch at a family tavern near the cathedral. Our neighbour recommended the grilled sardines, which should be in season
  from late spring.
- 15:00 walk up to the Miradouro de Santa Luzia for the view over the roofs and the river.
- 16:30 Sao Jorge castle. Tickets are 15 euros for adults and 7.50 euros for children from 6 to 12 years.
- 19:30 dinner at home; we will buy bread, cheese and fruit at the market on the way.

## Day 2, Friday: Belem

- 09:30 take tram 15 from Praca da Figueira to Belem, about 40 minutes. Go early because the queues grow after eleven.
- 10:30 Jeronimos Monastery. Entry is 12 euros for adults. The cloister is the highlight.
- 12:30 the famous custard tarts from the old bakery next door. Rui insists we try them warm with cinnamon.
- 14:00 Belem Tower from the outside only. The inside is small and Beatriz prefers the riverside lawn.
- 16:00 the Maritime Museum, which has real ship models and costs 10 euros for adults and nothing for children under 12.

## Day 3, Saturday: Sintra

- 08:30 train from Rossio station to Sintra, 40 minutes, about 2.40 euros each way. Trains leave every 20 minutes.
- 10:00 Pena Palace. Tickets are 20 euros for adults and 18 euros for children. The bus up the hill costs 3.50 euros; we will
  take it up and walk down through the park.
- 14:00 lunch in the old town, then a travesseiro pastry from the shop on the main street.
- 17:30 return to Lisbon.

## Day 4, Sunday: the flea market and home

The Feira da Ladra flea market runs on Tuesdays and Saturdays only, so we will skip it. Instead we will have a slow breakfast,
check out by 11:00 and take the metro to the airport. Our flight leaves at 15:50, and we want to be at the airport by 13:45.
