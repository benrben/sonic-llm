Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 91 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

The hall. The caretaker, Elena Wexler, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 91 chairs and 23 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 362 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 91 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 23 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Elena Wexler each spring.
