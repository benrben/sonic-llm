Correspondence. A letter arrived from Bluepine Logistics offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Viktor Rossetti, who lives in Calderholm, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Social events. The summer outing to Calderholm went well, with 4 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Viktor Rossetti suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

The hall. The caretaker, Viktor Rossetti, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 4 chairs and 85 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.
