Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 325 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 81 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 71 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Anouk Haddad each spring.

Social events. The summer outing to Thistlestead went well, with 81 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Anouk Haddad suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Wrenfield Foods offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Anouk Haddad, who lives in Thistlestead, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 81 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

The hall. The caretaker, Anouk Haddad, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 81 chairs and 71 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.
