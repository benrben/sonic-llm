Any difference between the first photo list and the final state will be discussed in a friendly way. The landlord keeps a list of about 76 recommended cleaners, and the tenant may use any of them or do the work personally.

Changes and repairs. The tenant may paint the walls in light colours if the flat is returned in white at the end. Broken glass from normal use is the tenant's cost.

Utilities and services. Water and the shared electricity in the hallway are included in the heating charge.
