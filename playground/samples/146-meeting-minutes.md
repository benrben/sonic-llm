Correspondence. A letter arrived from Applegate Engineering offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Hugo Yilmaz, who lives in Fenwickbridge, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 5 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 352 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 5 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 54 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Hugo Yilmaz each spring.

Social events. The summer outing to Fenwickbridge went well, with 5 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Hugo Yilmaz suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.
