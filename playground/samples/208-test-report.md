Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-2084 is 183 euros, not 100 euros.

Quality control. A duplicate of every 25th sample was measured again, and the results agreed within the allowed range. The balance was checked with a standard weight of 12 g before use. The distilled water used for rinsing comes from the laboratory tap, which is tested every month. Records of the checks are kept in the folder for this year.

Equipment and maintenance. The instrument was last serviced about 25 months ago and the probe was replaced at that time. The probe is stored in its special liquid between uses and never left to dry. If the readings begin to wander, the probe should be cleaned first and replaced second. The next service is planned for the autumn.

Safety. All the people in the room wore gloves, safety glasses and lab coats. The room was ventilated during the work, and the instrument was placed away from the sink. Waste liquids were poured into the labelled container and handed over to Ulrich Tavares, who is in charge of waste. No spills occurred, and no first aid was needed.
