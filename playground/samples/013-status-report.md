Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-5241 is 229 euros, not 477 euros.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Copperleaf Studio, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 56 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Communication. The weekly status is sent to the steering group every Friday. The team keeps a public board where everybody can see what is in progress. If something blocks the work for more than a day, it is raised in the daily check-in and, if needed, with the steering group. Next month we will hold a short review to see how the project is going overall.

Team notes. A new colleague, Jonas Whitlock, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 56 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.
