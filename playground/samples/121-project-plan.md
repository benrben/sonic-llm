Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-9904 is 322 euros, not 172 euros.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 16 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-385.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Saltmarsh Foods, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 16 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Dmitri Zielinski, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 16 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.
