Correspondence. A letter arrived from Quillon Studio offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Pavel Wexler, who lives in Harrowburg, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 745 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 58 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 75 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Pavel Wexler each spring.

Social events. The summer outing to Harrowburg went well, with 58 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Pavel Wexler suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.
