Allotment Circle of Marlowcross: minutes of the committee meeting

The committee met on January 20, 2027 at the hall on Station Lane, Holliscross. Esme Whitlock took the chair and Sven Salazar kept the minutes. 21 members came, which was well above the quorum of 4. Apologies were received from three people who could not travel. The minutes of the meeting held on December 7, 2026 were read, agreed as a true record and signed.

1. Matters arising
The clerk reported that the letter to the council about the hall booking had been sent and acknowledged. Nothing further was needed. The chair thanked everyone who helped to tidy the hall after the last event, and asked that the cupboard in room 3 be kept clear so that the cleaners can reach the sockets.

2. Discussion of the community open day
Members spoke for about forty minutes on the community open day. Several people felt that the plan was sound but that the timetable was too tight, and a smaller group offered to look at costs before the next meeting. The chair reminded the meeting that the club spent 622 euros last year on similar work and that this figure had been agreed as a guide rather than a limit. After a short vote the committee approved a budget of 3942 euros for the community open day, to be spent only against receipts.

3. Actions
Wim Kessler will collect three written quotes and send them to the clerk by February 1, 2027. The clerk will add the quotes to the next agenda. Nobody else needs to do anything before then. It was agreed that any member may ask to see the receipts at any time, and that the treasurer will keep them in the blue folder at the hall.

4. Any other business
A member asked whether the club could offer a short beginners' session in the spring. The chair said this was a good idea and asked for a volunteer to write a proposal; no one volunteered yet, so the item stays open. There was a brief discussion of the refreshments rota, which works well, and of the noticeboard, which needs a new lock.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Kestrel Tools offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Bastian Olsen, who lives in Kestrelford, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.

Social events. The summer outing to Kestrelford went well, with 39 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Bastian Olsen suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 397 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 39 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 30 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Bastian Olsen each spring.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 39 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

The meeting closed at ten past nine. The next meeting will be held on February 4, 2027.
