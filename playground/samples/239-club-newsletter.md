Sablestead Walking Club: the March newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Mira Olsen, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 268 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on January 6, 2027 at the Chapel Lane grounds in Lindenhaven. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 75 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 36 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by December 15, 2026 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 8, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 9 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Member profile. This month we meet Clara Abernethy, who joined the club 57 years ago and has not missed a single season since. Asked what keeps him coming back, he said that it is the people, the fresh air and the tea at the end. He has a favourite spot near the old tree, and he has promised to show new members around.

Lost and found. Over the summer the following items were left at the clubhouse: a blue water bottle, one glove, a pair of reading glasses and a folded umbrella. They are in a box by the door. Anything that is not collected within 57 weeks will go to the charity shop in Tarnbridge. Please look in the box, even if you think you have lost nothing.

Thank you. Special thanks to Clara Abernethy for painting the fence, to Leon Salazar for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 532 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Applegate Studio, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

Tips of the month. Bring a spare pair of dry socks, since the paths can be wet. Check the weather before you leave and tell somebody where you are going. Water is available at the clubhouse, but not along the way. If you are bringing a child, please let us know in advance so that we have enough helpers and equipment for the 57 youngest members.

This newsletter was put together by Karin Iversen. If you have an item for next time, send it in before the end of the month.
