Elmsteadstead Walking Club: the May newsletter

A word from the president
Dear members, welcome to another newsletter. Jonas Oyelaran, our president, writes: "The club has grown to 69 members this year, and I want to thank everyone who has helped with the boats, the paths and the kettle. A club is only as good as the people who turn up on a wet morning."

The big event
Our annual open day will take place on February 20, 2027 at the Foundry Lane grounds in Kestrelbridge. There will be short guided sessions for beginners, a tea tent, a book swap and a small raffle. The first prize in the raffle is a voucher worth 144 euros, donated by a local shop. Entry for members is free; guests pay 41 euros, which covers the tea and a place in one of the sessions.

To take part in a session, please sign up by January 29, 2027 using the list on the noticeboard or by sending a message to the club address. Places for the children's session are limited to 6, and they are given out in the order that names arrive. We still need 9 volunteers to help with parking, tea and tidying up, so if you have an hour to spare, please say so.

Club news
The winter repairs are finished and the shed has a new roof. The committee thanks the members who donated their weekends. The photo wall in the entrance has been refreshed with pictures from last year; if you spot one of yourself and would like a copy, just ask. New members are always welcome, and an introductory evening is held every first Thursday.

Reminders
Please return borrowed equipment cleaned and dry. Park on the side of the road marked for visitors so that the lane stays clear for deliveries. The kettle and the mugs are for everybody, and so is the washing up.

Tips of the month. Bring a spare pair of dry socks, since the paths can be wet. Check the weather before you leave and tell somebody where you are going. Water is available at the clubhouse, but not along the way. If you are bringing a child, please let us know in advance so that we have enough helpers and equipment for the 55 youngest members.

Lost and found. Over the summer the following items were left at the clubhouse: a blue water bottle, one glove, a pair of reading glasses and a folded umbrella. They are in a box by the door. Anything that is not collected within 55 weeks will go to the charity shop in Kestrelwick. Please look in the box, even if you think you have lost nothing.

Dates for the diary. The monthly walk starts at ten o'clock on the first Saturday and covers about 55 kilometres. The committee meets on the second Wednesday of the month. A repair weekend is planned for May 1, 2027, and a social evening for March 28, 2027. All the dates will be repeated on the noticeboard and on the club's web page, so please check them before you travel.

Thank you. Special thanks to Vera Thorne for painting the fence, to Esme Wexler for mending the gate and to everyone who brought cakes. The bake sale in the spring raised 979 euros, which will go toward new equipment. The club also received a kind letter from Lanternworks Supply, which has offered to give us a discount on supplies.

This newsletter was put together by Selma Lindqvist. If you have an item for next time, send it in before the end of the month.
