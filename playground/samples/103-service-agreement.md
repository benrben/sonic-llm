Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-1032 is 166 euros, not 262 euros.

Changes and repairs. The tenant may paint the walls in light colours if the flat is returned in white at the end. Drilling holes for shelves is allowed, but large nails and sticky hooks on tiles are not. The flat has 98 windows, all of which were checked by the landlord before the tenant moved in. Broken glass from normal use is the tenant's cost.

Utilities and services. Water and the shared electricity in the hallway are included in the heating charge. The tenant arranges and pays for their own electricity and internet, and must give the meter readings on the day they move in; the starting reading on the electricity meter was 426. Rubbish collection is on 98 days of the month, and bins must be put out the evening before.

Parking and storage. There is room for one bicycle per person in the shared room on the ground floor, and a small cellar compartment, number 98, belongs to the flat. No parking space is included, but residents may apply to the town for a permit. The landlord asks that nothing is stored on the stairs or in the corridor because of the fire rules.
