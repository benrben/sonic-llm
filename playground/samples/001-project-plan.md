Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of KX-3084 is 30 euros, not 355 euros.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Lanternworks Print, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 67 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Pavel Salazar, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 67 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 67 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-348.
