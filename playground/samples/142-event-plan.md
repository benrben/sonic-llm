Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-4110 is 148 euros, not 421 euros.

Wish list. If there is time we would like to see the lighthouse, walk the hill path to the ruined castle, and find a shop that sells local ceramics. Jonas Vasquez recommended the sunset from the old pier, and a small gallery that is open until 11 in the evening. We will decide day by day and not plan every hour.

Getting around. The old town is small and easy to walk. A day ticket for local buses costs 92 euros, and the ticket machine at the station takes cards. Bicycles can be rented near the harbour, but the hills are steeper than they look. Jonas Vasquez, who visited last year, warns that the cobbled streets are hard on suitcase wheels.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Wrenfield Print near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 92 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Thistlestead often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.
