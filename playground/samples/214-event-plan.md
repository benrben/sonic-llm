Getting around. The old town is small and easy to walk. A day ticket for local buses costs 82 euros, and the ticket machine at the station takes cards. Bicycles can be rented near the harbour, but the hills are steeper than they look. Dmitri Yilmaz, who visited last year, warns that the cobbled streets are hard on suitcase wheels.

Eating out. A colleague recommended a small restaurant called Tidewater Foods near the market, which has a short menu and fair prices. We expect to spend about 82 euros per person on a good dinner and much less on lunch. Cafes in Kestrelford often close early on Sundays, so we will buy bread and fruit the day before if we want a picnic.

Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 225, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.
