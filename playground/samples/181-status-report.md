Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of VN-2968 is 133 euros, not 56 euros.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 32 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-457.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Felix Quinlan, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 32 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Team notes. A new colleague, Felix Quinlan, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 32 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.
