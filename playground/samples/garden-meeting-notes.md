# Willow Court community garden: committee meeting notes

**Date:** Tuesday 12 March, 19:00 to 20:20
**Place:** the library meeting room, Willow Court
**Present:** Priya Nair (chair), Tomas Eklund (treasurer), Mei-Lin Chou (secretary), Joao Pereira, Hannah Brooks,
Samuel Oyelaran, and four residents from the waiting list as observers
**Apologies:** Dmitri Kovacs

## 1. Minutes of the last meeting

The minutes of the 13 February meeting were approved without changes. Mei-Lin will post them on the notice board by Friday.

## 2. Treasurer's report

Tomas reported a balance of 1,840 euros. Income since February was 360 euros from plot fees (24 plots at 15 euros each).
Spending was 215 euros: 120 euros for two cubic metres of compost, 65 euros for a replacement hose and 30 euros for
printing. The committee agreed to keep the plot fee at 15 euros for the coming season. Joao asked whether the garden could
apply for the city's small grants fund; Tomas will check the deadline, which he thinks is 30 April.

## 3. The water problem

The rain barrel by plot 7 has been leaking since January and the tap on the shed side gives only a trickle. Samuel looked at
it on Saturday and thinks the barrel's outlet fitting has cracked. A new fitting costs about 12 euros. The committee approved
spending up to 40 euros on repairs. Samuel will fix it before the first planting weekend. Hannah suggested adding a second
barrel behind the shed; the committee liked the idea but decided to wait until the grant answer is known.

## 4. Planting weekend

The spring planting weekend will be Saturday 6 April and Sunday 7 April, from 10:00 to 15:00. Priya will bring seed
potatoes and Hannah will organise a swap table for seedlings. Soup will be served at lunch on Saturday; Mei-Lin asked
everyone to bring their own bowl and spoon. Volunteers are needed to sign up for the compost turning shifts, one hour each.

## 5. Plot allocation

There are 31 people on the waiting list and four plots became free this winter. The committee agreed to offer the plots in
list order, and to ask each new gardener to attend one work morning in their first month. Two plots, number 12 and number 19,
will be reduced from 20 square metres to 10 square metres so that more families can have a start. Existing holders of those
plots were consulted and agreed.

## 6. Rules reminder

Dmitri sent a note, read out by the chair, asking everyone to cover compost bins and not to leave tools outside overnight,
because someone took two spades last month. Joao proposed a padlock for the shed; it was agreed, and the key will be kept by
three committee members.

## 7. Any other business

- The beehive proposal from Hannah was postponed until the autumn meeting because of insurance questions.
- A resident asked about a children's corner. Priya will draw a sketch for the next meeting.
