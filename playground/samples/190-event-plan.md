Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-6230 is 451 euros, not 257 euros.

Emergencies. The local number for the ambulance is 147, and the nearest pharmacy is two streets from the hotel. We have both taken out travel insurance, and a copy of the policy is saved in the shared folder. The bank card helpline is also written there. If one of us gets lost, we will meet at the fountain on the market square.

Getting around. The old town is small and easy to walk. A day ticket for local buses costs 19 euros, and the ticket machine at the station takes cards. Bicycles can be rented near the harbour, but the hills are steeper than they look. Bastian Castellan, who visited last year, warns that the cobbled streets are hard on suitcase wheels.

Wish list. If there is time we would like to see the lighthouse, walk the hill path to the ruined castle, and find a shop that sells local ceramics. Bastian Castellan recommended the sunset from the old pier, and a small gallery that is open until 9 in the evening. We will decide day by day and not plan every hour.
