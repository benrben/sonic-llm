Erratum

Erratum to the table: the unit price of RT-5352 is 181 euros, not 61 euros.

Costs. The incident used about 41 hours of engineering time in total, spread over several people. We offered no refunds, since nobody was charged for the time of the outage. The cost of the extra servers we started during the recovery was small, and they were shut down again the following day. The review meeting will take one hour.

Communication. The status page was updated three times, and each update was reviewed by Ilse Castellan before it was published. An internal message went to the wider team, and a short note was sent to customers who had contacted support. We believe the updates were honest and clear, although one of them used a technical term that customers did not know.

Earlier incidents. A similar problem occurred some months ago and was recorded as ticket RF-850. At that time the cause was different, but the symptoms were the same, and the runbook did not tell the engineer where to look. We should have connected the two events earlier, and the review will look at how to make older reports easier to find.
