Brewmate air purifier, model KX-886: user guide

Thank you for choosing the Brewmate air purifier. Please read this guide before you use it for the first time and keep it somewhere safe. The model number is printed on the label under the base; you will need it if you call the help line.

Setting up
Place the air purifier on a flat, dry surface at least 29 cm from walls and other appliances. Do not put it near a sink or on a soft cloth. Remove the stickers and the foam from the box, and wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Plug it into a standard wall socket. The air purifier draws 672 watts, so avoid sharing a single extension lead with other heavy appliances.

Everyday use
Press the round button once to start and once to stop. The light turns green while it is working and white when it is waiting. The tank holds 3 litres and should not be filled above the marked line. Switch it off and unplug it before you clean it, and let it cool for about 37 minutes after a long session. Never use harsh chemicals on the casing; mild soapy water is enough.

If something goes wrong
If the display shows E-25, the filter or the inlet is blocked: switch off, unplug, clean the filter under running water and try again. The code E-42 means that the unit has overheated and is cooling down; wait until the light stops flashing. The code E-31 means the tank is empty or has been removed. Other codes are explained on the support page.

To reset the air purifier to its factory settings, hold the round button for 9 seconds until the light blinks twice. This clears your saved preferences but does not affect the warranty.

Warranty and support
Your air purifier is covered by a warranty of 34 months from the date of purchase, as long as it has been used as described in this guide. Keep your receipt with this booklet. For help, call the support line on extension 442 during office hours, or write to the address in the box. Please have the model number ready. Repairs made by anyone other than an approved service centre are not covered.

Accessories. A spare filter costs 45 euros and can be ordered with the part code RF-834 from the shop or from any approved dealer. A carrying bag and a second power cord are also available. Only use accessories made for this product; others may not fit well and can cause damage. The packaging contains 85 small screws for the wall bracket and a short instruction sheet.

Frequently asked questions. Why does the product make a low humming sound? This is normal and comes from the motor. Why does it smell a little when new? A new product can give off a mild smell for the first 45 hours of use, which disappears with ventilation. Can I use it in a bathroom? We do not recommend this, because moisture and electricity do not mix well.

Safety. Use the product only indoors and only for the purpose described in this guide. Keep it out of the reach of children under 7 years. Do not let the cord hang over the edge of a table. If the cord or the plug is damaged, stop using the product and have it replaced by the service centre. Never open the casing yourself: there are no parts inside that you can repair, and it may void the warranty.

Cleaning and care. Clean the filter every 45 weeks, or sooner if you notice a change in performance. Do not put any part in the dishwasher unless it is marked as safe for it. Dry all parts completely before you put them back. If you will not use the product for a long time, store it in its box in a cool, dry place, with the cord wrapped loosely and not pulled tight.

Disposal
When the air purifier reaches the end of its life, take it to a local collection point for electrical items. Do not put it in the household bin.
