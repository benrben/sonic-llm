The hall. The caretaker, Anouk Tennant, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 22 chairs and 3 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 22 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 429 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 22 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 3 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Anouk Tennant each spring.

Correspondence. A letter arrived from Wrenfield Print offering a discount on printing. The clerk will keep it on file and the committee will look at it when the next newsletter is due. A thank-you card came from the family of a former member. A short email from Anouk Tennant, who lives in Ivoryridge, asked whether visitors from other clubs may join our outings; the chair will reply that they are welcome.
