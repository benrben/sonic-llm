Project Orchard: weekly status

Week ending January 20, 2027. Written by Nadia Eriksen, project manager, for the steering group.

Overall
The project is on track but needs attention in one area. 16 people worked on it this week. The team finished the planned work on the first screen and started on the second one. The mood in the team is good, and the daily check-in is working well.

Money
So far 79000 euros of the total budget of 372000 euros have been spent. This is in line with the plan for this point in time. No extra spending has been approved. The finance team will send the next report at the end of the month.

Milestones
The next milestone, the first version for real users, is due on March 17, 2027. The team believes this is still realistic. The milestone after it is not yet scheduled, and will be discussed once the first version is in the hands of users.

Quality
96 bugs have been closed since the project began. 38 bugs are still open, and none of them stops people from using the product. The most serious open bugs are a layout problem on small screens and a slow search. Clara Rasmussen is working on both and expects to finish them next week.

Risks and blockers
One blocker is open: we are waiting for the data export. Irene Yilmaz is the owner of this blocker and has been chasing it for several days. If it is not solved soon, the milestone may move by a week or two. The steering group is asked to help by making a quick decision when the answer arrives.

Dependencies. The project depends on two other teams. The first, led by Elena Wexler, provides the data and has promised an update in a few days. The second provides the login service, which has been stable for 61 weeks. If either of them changes its plan, we will tell the steering group at once, because a delay could affect the date of the first release.

Feedback. Early users told us that the first screen is clear, but the buttons on the second are too small. We will fix this in the next sprint. One user, who works at Birchmoor Studio, asked for an export to a spreadsheet, and the team will consider it after the first version. In total 61 people have given feedback so far, and most comments were positive.

Decisions made this week. The team decided to keep the old colour scheme for the first version and to postpone the dark mode to a later release. It also decided to use the existing hosting service, which saves about 61 days of work. The decisions were taken by consensus, and nobody asked for a vote. They are recorded in the decision log under RF-498.

Team notes. A new colleague, Elena Wexler, joined the team on Monday and is already settling in. The team will move to a larger room next month. A short workshop on testing was held on Wednesday and 61 people attended. The team agreed to keep the daily check-in to fifteen minutes and to write down the decisions in the shared notes.

Next week
Finish the second screen, fix the layout bug, write the user guide and run a first test with three friendly users. Nadia Eriksen will send a short note on Friday to say how it went.
