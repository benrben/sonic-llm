Allotment Circle of Lindenvale: minutes of the committee meeting

The committee met on April 3, 2027 at the hall on Beacon Lane, Fenwickbridge. Tomas Ferreira took the chair and Vera Marchetti kept the minutes. 10 members came, which was well above the quorum of 4. Apologies were received from three people who could not travel. The minutes of the meeting held on February 21, 2027 were read, agreed as a true record and signed.

1. Matters arising
The clerk reported that the letter to the council about the hall booking had been sent and acknowledged. Nothing further was needed. The chair thanked everyone who helped to tidy the hall after the last event, and asked that the cupboard in room 6 be kept clear so that the cleaners can reach the sockets.

2. Discussion of the new storage shed
Members spoke for about forty minutes on the new storage shed. Several people felt that the plan was sound but that the timetable was too tight, and a smaller group offered to look at costs before the next meeting. The chair reminded the meeting that the club spent 721 euros last year on similar work and that this figure had been agreed as a guide rather than a limit. After a short vote the committee approved a budget of 1476 euros for the new storage shed, to be spent only against receipts.

3. Actions
Greta Thorne will collect three written quotes and send them to the clerk by April 14, 2027. The clerk will add the quotes to the next agenda. Nobody else needs to do anything before then. It was agreed that any member may ask to see the receipts at any time, and that the treasurer will keep them in the blue folder at the hall.

4. Any other business
A member asked whether the club could offer a short beginners' session in the spring. The chair said this was a good idea and asked for a volunteer to write a proposal; no one volunteered yet, so the item stays open. There was a brief discussion of the refreshments rota, which works well, and of the noticeboard, which needs a new lock.

Welfare. The committee agreed to send flowers to a member who is recovering from an operation and to visit on the following weekend. About 5 cards were signed at the last event and will be posted. The chair asked everyone to keep an eye on older members who may find the winter hard, and to pass on any worries quietly to the clerk.

Social events. The summer outing to Maplebridge went well, with 5 people on the coach and good weather all day. A short quiz evening is planned for the dark months. Celia Kowalczyk suggested a joint evening with a neighbouring group, and members liked the idea, although nothing was fixed. Cake, tea and a small raffle are expected, as always.

The hall. The caretaker, Celia Kowalczyk, asked the committee to remember that the hall is not free on Tuesdays and that it holds 5 chairs and 8 tables. A leaking tap in the kitchen has been mended. The heating will be switched on from the second week of October, and the thermostat must not be touched by anyone except the caretaker, whatever the temperature in the room.

Treasurer's note. The treasurer reported 750 euros in the current account at the end of last month and a further 5 euros in the cash box. Membership fees have been collected from 8 households so far, and a reminder will go to the rest. Nothing unusual was spent in the quarter. The treasurer asked members to hand in receipts within a fortnight, because the accounts are checked by Celia Kowalczyk each spring.

The meeting closed at ten past nine. The next meeting will be held on May 6, 2027.
